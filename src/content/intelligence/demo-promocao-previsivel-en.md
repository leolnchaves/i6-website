## Why it matters now

Three forces push the decision into predictive territory:

1. **Volatility** — recent history no longer describes the next quarter.
2. **Granularity** — deciding on the network average costs margin at every edge.
3. **Cadence** — the decision has to fit a weekly routine, not an annual project.

## Where traditional approaches fail

Spreadsheets and fixed rules hold while variability is low. Once it rises, the fixed rule becomes a systematic error — always in the same direction.

> "The gain does not come from the most complex model, it comes from a decision finally made every day with the same criterion."
