## Direct answer

Most traffic is not logged in. Propensity models built on real-time session signals recover much of the lost personalization without requiring an account.

## The size of the problem

Across projects mapped by infinity6, **propensity** accounts for a material share of the result — and is almost always handled with averages, spreadsheets and fixed rules that fail to track real business volatility.

Three drivers explain most of the loss:

1. **Scattered data** across ERP, POS and digital channels, with no single decision view.
2. **Static rules** that never recompute when demand shifts levels.
3. **Slow decision cycles**, where information arrives after the window to act.

## Why traditional approaches fail

Conventional tools describe the past reasonably well, but they do not estimate the uncertainty range of the future. Without it, every inventory, price or mix decision is made at the mean — precisely the scenario that almost never happens.
