## Direct answer

A 40-page guide covering data, modeling and governance to take demand forecasting from pilot to production in chains with more than 200 stores.

## The size of the problem

Across projects mapped by infinity6, **demand** accounts for a material share of the result — and is almost always handled with averages, spreadsheets and fixed rules that fail to track real business volatility.

Three drivers explain most of the loss:

1. **Scattered data** across ERP, POS and digital channels, with no single decision view.
2. **Static rules** that never recompute when demand shifts levels.
3. **Slow decision cycles**, where information arrives after the window to act.

## Why traditional approaches fail

Conventional tools describe the past reasonably well, but they do not estimate the uncertainty range of the future. Without it, every inventory, price or mix decision is made at the mean — precisely the scenario that almost never happens.

## The infinity6 proprietary engine

The **i6Previsio**, **i6RecSys** and **i6ElasticPrice** engines work together: probabilistic forecasting, contextual recommendation and price elasticity feed the same decision layer, refreshed daily and integrated over API.
